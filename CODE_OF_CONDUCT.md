# Code of conduct

gx follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, discussions, pull requests, and anywhere else you
represent the project.

In short: be respectful and assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks,
and discriminatory language are not acceptable. Disagree about code, not people.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior breaks this code, contact the maintainer,
[@joelachance](https://github.com/joelachance), privately. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block
contributors who violate the code, following the enforcement guidelines in the
Contributor Covenant.
